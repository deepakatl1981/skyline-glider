Privacy Policy — Skyline Glider

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Skyline Glider is a single-player arcade game. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind.

What the app stores

The game saves your progress on your own device using Android's standard SharedPreferences storage. That data never leaves your phone and is not readable by us or anyone else. It consists of:

  • Your coin balance and lifetime coins earned
  • Best score, farthest distance and total runs played
  • Which gliders you have unlocked and which one is selected
  • Mission progress and daily reward streak
  • Your sound and haptics preferences

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. You can also clear it at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Skyline Glider → Storage → Clear data.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is requested or collected — no name, email, phone number or address
  • No location data is accessed
  • No contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone access
  • No advertising identifiers are read, and no advertising SDKs are included
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs are included
  • No data is transmitted off the device, so nothing is sold or shared with third parties

Permissions

The app requests a single permission, android.permission.VIBRATE, used only to provide haptic feedback during gameplay. You can turn haptics off from the main menu. This permission grants no access to any data.

Children

Skyline Glider is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data whatsoever, it collects nothing from children either. It complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) by virtue of collecting no personal data.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds anything that collects data — for example advertising or online leaderboards — this policy will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here.
