## Full description (4000 max)

```
The city ends at the rooftop. Keep going anyway.

Skyline Glider is an endless runner across a neon-dusk skyline. Sprint the rooftops, vault the gaps, and when the roof runs out — snap open your wingsuit and fly.

SIMPLE TO PLAY, HARD TO MASTER
Swipe left and right between three rooftop lanes. Swipe up to jump. Swipe up AGAIN in mid-air and your wingsuit opens, turning a jump into a long, floating glide. That second swipe is the whole game: a plain jump clears any gap, but only a proper glide reaches the coin arcs strung high over the void.

THE ROOFTOPS FIGHT BACK
• Patrol drones sweeping across the lanes at chest height — slide under them or glide over
• Crosswind zones that shove you sideways until you lean back into the gust
• Scaffolding bars you must slide beneath
• Water towers and antennas that leave you exactly one safe lane
• Gaps where the rooftop simply stops

POWER-UPS
Magnet pulls every coin to you. Shield absorbs one crash. Speed Boost turns the skyline into streaks of light.

KEEP COMING BACK
• Six unlockable gliders, each with their own colours
• Rotating missions that carry over between runs and get harder as you clear them
• Seven-day daily reward streak
• Second Wind — spend your coins for one more chance at a great run

NO NONSENSE
No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No internet connection required. No data collected — nothing you do in this game leaves your phone. Just the game.

Built in Kotlin with Jetpack Compose. Every building, character, coin and sound effect is generated procedurally in code — there isn't a single image file in the app.

Playable one-handed. Runs on Android 7.0 and up.
```

*~1,570 characters.*

## Target audience and content

You chose **all ages, including children**, which puts the app in Google Play's
Families programme. That's viable here precisely because the game has no ads, no
purchases and no data collection — the three things that make Families
compliance painful. You'll need to:

- Select age groups including under-13 in *Target audience and content*
- Confirm the app is designed for children
- Confirm no ads and no data collection (both already true)

If you'd rather skip the extra scrutiny, select 13+ instead. The game's content
rating doesn't change either way.
